My introduction

Hi, I’m Siva Kumar Bokam — an AI Engineer with around four years of overall professional experience and a strong focus on Data Science, Machine Learning and Generative AI.

I enjoy taking an idea from problem definition to a working application. My hands-on interests include building RAG pipelines, LLM-powered workflows, agentic applications and Python APIs—from extracting information from complex documents to making business data easier to explore through natural language.

I like understanding how each part works: how information is prepared, how relevant context is retrieved, how an LLM makes use of it, and how the final output is validated and delivered through an application.

Beyond the code, I value clear problem-solving, continuous learning and building systems that are useful, dependable and easier for people to work with.
